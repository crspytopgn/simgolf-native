# Playing the club

Run `simgolf --game "../game/Program_Files_(ENGLISH)"`. It opens on the title menu, drawn from the disc's own Interface art with the game's font: Start New Game (Standard) or Sandbox Mode opens the property chooser, and picking a property starts a course on that property's theme. A property is paid for out of the §100,000 starting funds (sandbox: unlimited). Esc goes back, and from the course returns to the menu. Continue Saved Game loads the saved game (`course.sgs`), or the saved course (`course.sgc`) when there is no saved game.

For scripted runs (any of `--png`, `--course`, `--edit`, `--golfer`, `--sandbox`, `--follow`) the game skips the menu and opens on the demo course; `--screen menu|property|play` forces a screen. Add `--theme Desert` for another look there. `--property NAME` (or 0..15) starts a new game on that property straight away, and `--clock MS` fixes the value the exe's random generator is seeded from, so the same land comes back every run.

- **Holes** are built one at a time with the exe's rules (docs/GAMELOGIC.md): paint one Tee tile (G twice, or the Paint tool) and a Putting Green, then press H or click the cup green to open the hole. Its par comes from its planned length. Painting over a hole's tee or cup closes that hole until it is rebuilt. Courses made outside the tool (the demo course, saved terrain) have their tee and green pairs turned into holes when they load. Shift+H hides the advisor.
- **Golfers** follow the exe's rules (docs/GAMELOGIC.md): invited members come in pairs, walk and play every hole, use the amenities, pay a green fee equal to their mood at the end of each hole, and quit for good when their mood falls below zero. Happy members upgrade their membership and bring friends.
- **Fun** sums each hole's mood changes the way the exe does. Happy golfers pay more, unhappy ones less.
- **Money:** you start with §100,000 (confirmed from the publisher's golf.exe and the property screen). The green fee defaults to 1000 per hole, from the scale seen in course reports. Upkeep and wages are charged every game day (120 seconds). When cash stays below zero the board sends warnings and then ends the game, in four steps (the days are placeholders). Sandbox mode never charges.
- **The land** of a new game is built the way the exe builds it: each game deals the properties into sixteen offer slots (which set the acreage and price shown on the chooser), and the land generator lays out trees, creek, coast, ponds and rocks, wildlife spots, the clubhouse, the property's own feature and the difficulty's obstacles, and leaves the land beyond the purchased acreage out of bounds (not drawn) or, on an island, under water. Landmarks, obstacles and free buildings are drawn with the exe's art for the theme. Your own pro starts at the clubhouse and, until the first hole opens, follows the pointer as the exe's pro does; hired staff (People button) work around their post the way the exe's staff do: the Soda Vendor sells drinks to thirsty golfers, the Club Pro chats to golfers, the Ranger hurries them, the Groundskeeper pulls weeds. Click an employee on the course, then click where they should work, to move their post (the scripted hook `--edit "h:KIND,X,Y"` hires one with a post). Weeds grow and golfers complain about grown ones (-2 mood). The console prints events (strokes, fees, board messages).

- **Course Report:** press F1 on the course. It uses the disc's report art and lists every hole: yards, par, average strokes, time, fun, the +Len/+Acc/+Img scores (measured by simulating golfers with and without each skill, as the original's Shot Analysis suggests), type, average fee, revenue and profit. Yards, minutes and the profit split are placeholder scales. Esc, F1 or a click closes it. `--screen report` opens it directly.

Saving: Shift+S saves the whole game (land, golfers, holes, staff, money, calendar and the random generator, so it continues exactly) to `course.sgs` and the course to `course.sgc`; Shift+L loads them. Test hooks: `--save-game F` saves after the `--time` simulation, `--load-game F` starts from a saved game. `--cash N` sets the starting cash, `--time S` simulates S seconds before taking `--png`, `--edit "t:x,y,type;o"` paints one tile through the hole tool (type 0 tee, 1 green) and opens the hole being built.

## Buildings and garden items

In edit mode (Tab), press T until the title shows "Building", then [ and ] choose: benches, flower beds, ball washers, willow trees and scenic bridges at any time, the amenities as holes unlock them. Prices, clearing charges, upgrades and the path-to-clubhouse rule follow the exe. Benches need rough ground with a free neighbour; bridges go on water near a shore. Shift-click undoes: a path, bench, flower bed, willow or bridge comes off with what it cost back, a building is demolished with no refund. Golfers use what you build: snack bars and the practice buildings earn money on every visit, tired golfers sit on benches, flower beds and landmarks cheer them up. The headless test option is `--edit "b:x,y,kind"`.

## In-game interface (dock, panels, advisor, stories)

The lower left of the course screen now shows the game's own dock art (Interface/3mainLowerLeft.pcx). The three big round buttons open panels along the bottom: **Build Course** (terrain brushes, paths, raise and lower, each with its cost), **Add Buildings** (the amenities for the theme) and **People** (click to hire, right click to fire a club pro, ranger, groundskeeper or soda vendor). Click an item, then click the map. The small buttons zoom, rotate, open the course report, pause, and save the course. Keyboard shortcuts still work.

The advisor box at the top gives hints based on the state of your club, written by me, not taken from the game; press Shift+H to hide it. Golfer stories play as in the original: a pair that fits a story from your Themes folder gets it at the first tee, and its lines appear under the advisor as the two walk together; good moods carry it to a Happy Ending, which unlocks a landmark. Special visitors (the Corporate CEO, the County commissioner, the Wealthy heiress) come once your ratings earn them; please them and they invest, approve a land expansion (the TRACTS FOR SALE screen opens) or donate a landmark. Donated and owned landmarks can be placed with the building tool. Story text is read from your disc at run time and is not stored in this project.

## Your pro (Gary Golf)

Open the **People** panel: its first rows are your pro's. **Practice Round** (or J) sends him out with a partner; **Match** (or Shift+J) is lit once a famous golfer has challenged your club, which happens from the second year on when a newcomer with all three skills arrives and you have enough holes. Before his first round you hand out ten skill points (plus three for each accomplishment already earned) in the skill dialog: + and - on each row, then Done. K opens the dialog again; points won later (his first skill gain, his first match win) are added there.

On each full shot away from the green the game waits for you: move the pointer over the course to aim (the white line shows the shot, bending for a draw or fade; the box sits on the aimed tile or its nearest corner) and click to swing. The panel offers the five shot types (S straight, F fade, D draw, H high backspin, L low punch); fade, draw and high are refused from a hazard. The box on the right shows his club, attitude (his momentum), distance and lie, with the skills that count for this shot in white. The computer plays his putts and short chips. N, pressed twice, cancels the round; his partner plays on alone.

A match is played for the wager level's stake on each hole and again on the match; losing or tying lowers the stake for the next challenge. Remarkable shots, judged the way the exe judges them, raise one of his skills (up to a cap set by the course's rank); finishing shots in hazards may cost him one. Scripted runs: `--edit "j:0"` starts a practice round and lets him aim at the pin by himself (and take up challenges); `j:0,1` only shows the aim, for stills; `--screen skills` opens the skill dialog.

## SGA tournaments

At the start of the fifth month each year the SGA looks at your course (when your pro is not out). If it passes, the SGA offers a tournament and the People panel shows **Begin Tournament**. Clicking it evaluates the course again: a failing course shows the SGA report (ten criteria scored out of 10, with what the SGA wants for your course's class: length, number of holes, time to play, fun, variety, scenic holes, holes that test length, accuracy and imagination, and facilities joined to the clubhouse); a passing one shows the event, its first prize and **Great, let the games begin!**.

The course empties and a shotgun field starts: two players on each hole, your pro on hole 1 with a famous partner, everyone else a famous pro picked to suit the purse and your cash. A checklist follows: TV towers and booths, extra smooth greens, deeper rough, and each hole's tournament par; untick what you don't want. The leaderboard sits on the left while they play (your pro in gold). When the last player is in, the results show each place's prize (the purse for first, two thirds of the place before for each place after, for as many places as you have holes); your pro's prize goes to the club, and places bring trophies. The towers come down, the old golfers come back, and two regular golfers start on each hole. N twice cancels a tournament with no prizes. Scripted runs: `--edit "g:0"` asks the SGA (the evaluation must pass), `g:1` starts one straight away with the default purse; `--screen sga` shows the report.

## Play a Championship

In a game, Shift+F7 saves your course for championship play and Shift+F8 saves your pro (into a `Championship` folder next to your saves; pro files use the exe's layout, so the disc's `Themes/Championship/Gary Golf.pro` is listed too). From the title menu, **Play a Championship** lets you pick a difficulty, a course and a pro, then plays a tournament on it straight away: $100,000 in cash, June, every preparation item taken, the event named by difficulty (SGA Qualifying School to SGA Open Championship), no wages and no accomplishments. When the results are closed you are back at the title menu; nothing is saved. Scripted runs: `--champ COURSE.sgch PRO.pro`, and `--edit "x"` saves the course.

## Life on the course

- **Thoughts:** every mood event shows a line over the golfer who says it (or the partner, for remarks about the golfer), white then grey as it fades, green or red by its tone. Lines start with the words the exe's labels show; the rest is written for this port, since the exe's strings are data.
- **Wildlife:** when you retype the land, an animal whose habitat and theme fit a tile next to it may move in with a young one (elk, cranes, flamingos, sheep, crocodiles, snakes, gila monsters, roadrunners, ducks). They wander, graze or drink, stay in their habitat and run from golfers and low balls; a golfer who startles one is pleased on the easier difficulties. Which kind lives where is our choice (the exe's table is data). A hot-air balloon, a sailboat, a bird of prey and a blimp pass over now and then.
- **Water:** a ball in the water splashes; ripples line the shores, rocks and coral sit in the shallows, and scenic water has dolphins or a fountain.
- **Celebrity homes:** a home site bought by a celebrity becomes one of two celebrity houses, and the celebrity walks about the lot and now and then does a signature move.
- Test hooks: `a:x,y,kind` places an animal, `y:x,y` makes a celebrity home.

## The clubhouse and the records

- Click the clubhouse to choose who tees off next: the golfers waiting are shown newest first; pick two and click outside the cards (no picks cancels).
- People panel, **Golfers on the course**: every golfer playing (hole, name coloured by thirst, hunger, tiredness or anger, and mood). Click one to centre on them and open their card: the five bars (a long bar is bad), the scorecard against par and recent thoughts; X closes it.
- **F10** shows the Professional Accomplishments board, which also opens by itself after each of the 22 accomplishments (from the first Challenge hole to the 100 star rating): a photo card for each, the plaques on the trophy and the next three things to do.
- At each new year the year-end report compares cash, fun, skill and membership with last year and lists the year's highlights from the event log (holes opened, buildings built, matches won, top-rated holes, celebrity homes, tournament places, Happy Endings, land bought).
- **F9** shows the Membership Roster; **F7** shows the SGA evaluation. `--screen pair|board|yearend|roster|golfers` opens them for stills.
