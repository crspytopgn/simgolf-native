# Code of Conduct

OpenSGolf follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, criticise work and not people, and do not harass anyone. Harassment,
insults, discrimination and sharing other people's private information are not accepted anywhere the project meets (issues,
pull requests, chat, forums).

Report problems to the maintainers by opening a private security advisory on GitHub or by email to the address in the
repository's profile. Reports are handled in confidence. Maintainers may remove content and ban people who break these rules.
