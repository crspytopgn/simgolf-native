Sid Meier's SimGolf

A native port of SimGolf that runs in your browser, using the game files from your own copy of SimGolf.

  1. Click the button below.
  2. Pick your SimGolf game folder: the one that contains Flics, Sounds and Data (on Windows usually C:\Program Files\Maxis\SimGolf or similar). Picking a folder above it works too.
  3. Your browser may ask to confirm reading the folder ("upload" in its wording): the files are only read inside this tab, nothing is sent anywhere.

Your game files are already in this browser.

The game's art, sounds and data belong to Electronic Arts and are not included here, which is why the page needs your copy. Works in desktop Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Sound starts after your first click. Saved courses stay in this browser. Source and notes: github.com/crspytopgn/simgolf-native.
